# LIQWD Playbook System — Authority Set: Deep Review & Adaptation Blueprint

**Purpose:** Dissect the 10 Search Atlas Authority/Digital PR/Link Building playbooks and adapt them for LIQWD. This is the set the Explorer blueprint's E6 (Authority Action Plan) anticipated: it contains both the machinery LIQWD must **refuse to copy** (deployed link networks) and the machinery that becomes LIQWD's **actual growth engine** (outreach infrastructure). Companion to the six prior blueprints; spec standard v1.1 applies.

**The governing split.** Every playbook here is some mix of two things: (a) *analysis and outreach machinery* — competitor authority intelligence, publisher discovery, personalized email sequencing, press distribution — which is legitimate and often excellent; and (b) *deployed-link acquisition* — Cloud Stack orders (15 HyperDrive credits → 14 "structured backlinks" on cloud platforms), Link Labs paid guest-post placements, press releases framed as link building. Purchased placements and entity-stack networks are link schemes under Google's spam policies. For a marketplace whose entire business is compounding organic trust, that's not a gray area to manage — it's an existential risk to decline, plus a brand risk (LIQWD's name on spammy web-2.0 properties). The adaptation rule for this whole set: **keep the intelligence and the outreach engines, replace every deployed-link channel with earned channels, and salvage the safety mechanics** (velocity control, anchor distribution, cost gates) as monitors on the earned profile.

---

## Part 1 — New Mechanics Extracted (Additions to the Library)

- **Sender-reputation protection** (Earn Editorial Backlinks): email verification before campaign start, smart daily sending limits, weekday-only schedules, inbox assignment confirmation. Deliverability treated as a guarded asset, same class as ad-account trust or GBP suspension defense.
- **Own-domain exclusion** (Launch Prospect Outreach): the prospect list is automatically filtered to remove the client's own domain — "prevents embarrassing outreach errors." Trivial, mandatory, and the kind of detail that separates production tools from demos.
- **Industry inference + confirmation gate** (Prospect Outreach): before mass processing, inferred prospect classifications are presented as a summary table for explicit user confirmation. Bulk-operation ambiguity resolution — the outreach twin of the ads suite's mapping-confirmation gate.
- **Per-prospect insight table** (Prospect Outreach): a strategic insight summary is compiled per prospect and *referenced in the messaging* — personalization grounded in researched fact, not mail-merge tokens.
- **Industry language pass** (Prospect Outreach): a dedicated second-pass rewrite into the prospect's professional vocabulary and realistic buyer behavior. Voice adaptation as an explicit pipeline stage.
- **No-false-claims QA** (Prospect Outreach): a named QA node verifying "no false audits or exaggerated claims" before approval. An honesty lint on outbound messaging — directly adoptable, and directly extensible with CASL/RECO checks (Part 3).
- **Value-first, non-promotional email doctrine** (both Digital PR tools): 3-step sequences leading with value to the recipient; promotional asks subordinated. Encoded as a generation rule, not a style suggestion.
- **Reply drafting on response** (Earn Editorial Backlinks): monitoring surfaces replies immediately and drafts responses for human send. The machine never converses autonomously with a journalist.
- **Keyword-first publisher discovery** (Launch Publisher Outreach): instead of chasing a single URL's backlinks, discover publishers *ranking* for high-intent keywords (listicles, resource roundups, active publications) — outreach targets selected by demonstrated relevance and reach.
- **Route-to-specialist delegation + plan-only mode** (Create Press Release): the playbook can hand off to a dedicated specialist agent, or produce a recommended plan (angle, content type, cost) *without executing*. "Plan-only" as a first-class run mode joins the spec standard.
- **Cost approval before credit spend** (Press Release; Cloud Stack; Guest Post): every credit-consuming action presents cost and secures explicit approval first — the unit-priced-consent pattern, now standard across content credits too.
- **Anchor & velocity control as an ongoing monitor** (30/90-Day Plans; New-Site Authority): balanced anchor-text distribution and controlled link velocity as continuously watched health metrics. Salvaged from the deployed-link context and reapplied to LIQWD's *earned* profile.
- **Vulnerability targeting** (Close Competitor Gaps): find signals *missing* from competitor profiles and exploit the gaps — attack where they're absent, not just imitate where they're present.

## Part 2 — Playbook-by-Playbook Review & Triage

**1 · Digital PR — Launch Publisher Outreach → P1.** DAG: generate 5–15 high-intent buyer keywords + define project context (client authority, targeting parameters) → run keyword search (domains ranking in top 20) + filter opportunities (listicles, resource roundups, active publications) → configure campaign + select outreach mode (editorial inclusion vs HARO-style expert quote) → draft 3-step value-first email sequence + prepare publication-ready expert quote → compliance audit (editorial tone, domain relevance, setup rules) → final deliverables. *Keep nearly whole.* This is white-hat when the ask is genuine editorial inclusion: for LIQWD, the targets are "best pre-construction platforms GTA"-style listicles, Toronto real-estate roundups, and journalist queries where LIQWD (or its verified agents) supply expert quotes — exactly the citation surface the AI Visibility blueprint's V3 routes to.

**2 · Digital PR — Earn Editorial Backlinks → P1.** DAG: analyze domain (business focus, content themes, topical authority) → campaign setup + email verification → source publications → contact scraping → filter & prioritize by DR/DA and traffic → draft outreach → user approval → configure settings (daily limits, weekday schedule) → launch → track performance → draft replies for human send. The operational half of P1: discovery-to-inbox infrastructure with the full sender-reputation and approval-gate discipline. Merge with #1 into one engine (two targeting modes: keyword-first; publication-first).

**3 · Digital PR — Launch Prospect Outreach → P2 (repurposed — the strategic find of the set).** DAG: collect & validate URLs (dedupe, exclude own domain) → industry inference (Local/Multi-location/National) → confirmation gate → initialize campaign + map target domains → per-prospect strategic analysis (local visibility, AI search impact, business risks) → compile insight table → draft 3-email sequence from the client's perspective focused on revenue impact → industry language pass → no-false-claims QA → launch readiness (inbox assignment, final deliverables for approval). In the source this sells SEO services. For LIQWD it is, almost unmodified, the **Realtor Recruitment Engine** — the growth tool LIQWD's own GTM needs: prospect realtor lists → per-prospect insight grounded in data LIQWD uniquely has (their Map Pack position via G1-class scans, what AI says about them via the V-suite tracker, their neighbourhood coverage gaps) → personalized value-first sequences → human-approved sends. Secondary mode: builder/developer partnership outreach for platform inventory. This one tool converts the whole dissection project into pipeline.

**4 · Authority Building — Create Press Release → P3.** DAG: confirm project & URL + identify the newsworthy announcement with the user → route to specialist / request plan only → present plan & cost → secure approval → generate draft → review content → submit release to networks → report results (preview links, networks, dashboard tracking). *Keep with honest reframing:* press-release syndication links are not an authority strategy (networks are largely nofollow and Google discounts them), but distribution of *real news* — platform launches, market reports, partnerships — is legitimate brand communication, and press coverage is a first-class citation source for AI answers (V-suite tie-in). P3 is a comms tool with plan-only mode and cost gates, never sold internally as link building.

**5 · Authority Building — Close Competitor Gaps → P4/E6.** DAG: gap diagnosis (competitor DR/DP, referring domains, backlink types + anchor & placement audit + topical cluster mapping) → strategy formulation (signal replication, vulnerability targeting, relevance optimization) → acquisition planning (Cloud Stacking, PR distribution, contextual placements, keyword-supporting links). *Split:* the entire diagnosis + strategy layer is excellent competitor intelligence — absorbed into P4. The acquisition layer is the deployed-link menu — replaced wholesale.

**6 · Authority Building — Create Backlink Strategy → P4/E6.** DAG: audit authority metrics + competitor benchmarking → determine link mix (Cloud Stacks, PRs, placements) + anchor & pacing plan + budget allocation → confirm budget & plan → automated rollout → track performance. Same split: the planning *structure* (phased plan, pacing discipline, budget consciousness, explicit sign-off) survives; the link-mix menu does not.

**7 · Authority Building — 30-Day Growth Plan → P4/E6 (structure only).** Domain metrics analysis → 2–4 target URLs → deploy Cloud Stacks → press release distribution → 4–5 editorial placements (DR 40–60+) → anchor & velocity control → growth roadmap. Salvage: the tight-scope sprint format (few URLs, one month, explicit velocity control) and the anchor/velocity monitor.

**8 · Authority Building — 90-Day Growth Plan → P4/E6 (structure only).** Baseline audit + competitor benchmarking → Phase 1 foundation (cloud stacks + initial PR) → Phase 2 topical depth (editorial placements + digital PR outreach) → Phase 3 expansion (second PR + additional placements via competitive modeling) → anchor diversification + timed roadmap. Salvage: the three-phase quarterly arc — foundation → topical depth → expansion — mapped onto earned channels.

**9 · Authority Building — Build New-Site Authority → skip (mechanics salvaged).** Scan site + target striking-distance pages → build cloud profiles + "clean, instantly-indexable" structured backlinks → press release + syndicated brand mentions → editorial placements + link safety controls (conservative velocity, safe anchor ratios for new domains). The purpose (bootstrap trust for a new domain) is real — LIQWD's answer is different: entity clarity via schema + llms.txt (V2), consistent NAP/citations where legitimate (G2's citation hygiene), real coverage via P1/P3, and patience. The new-domain conservatism principle (gentler velocity expectations on young properties) carries into P4's monitors.

**10 · Authority Building — Deploy Cloud Stack Order + Link Building — Launch Guest Post Campaign → skip entirely.** The pure purchase paths: one Cloud Stack order = 15 credits = 14 backlinks with per-site upsells; Link Labs guest-post placements pulled from Brand Vault with budget approval, order tracking, and placement logs. Cleanly built, well-gated — and exactly the machinery LIQWD must not own. Salvaged patterns only: mandatory cost approval before spend, placement documentation/audit trails (reapplied to earned coverage logging), niche-exclusion lists (reapplied to outreach targeting).

## Part 3 — The LIQWD Authority Suite (3 net-new tools + 1 upgrade)

- **P1 · Digital PR — Earned Editorial Engine** *(Admin)*. Merge of #1 + #2. Two targeting modes: **keyword-first** (publishers ranking for high-intent GTA real-estate terms; listicle/roundup inclusion; expert-quote placement) and **publication-first** (sourced publication lists, contact discovery, DR/traffic prioritization). Value-first 3-step sequences → human approval → rate-limited weekday sending on protected infrastructure → reply surfacing with drafted responses, human send. No-false-claims lint + RECO lint on every draft. Success metric: earned citations and coverage — which feed the V-suite's AI-visibility flywheel and E6's authority plan.
- **P2 · Outreach — Realtor Recruitment Engine** *(Admin — LIQWD's own growth tool)*. #3 repurposed: validated prospect lists (own-domain + existing-client exclusion) → industry/segment inference with confirmation gate → per-prospect insight table built from LIQWD's proprietary lenses (Map Pack position, AI-visibility snapshot, neighbourhood coverage gaps) → 3-email value-first sequence in realtor language → no-false-claims QA → **CASL compliance lint (see foundations — this is the make-or-break addition)** → launch readiness → human-approved sends → reply triage with drafted responses. Mode B: builder/developer partnership outreach. The insight table is the moat: no other outreach tool can tell a realtor what AI says about them.
- **P3 · Comms — Press Release Studio** *(Admin)*. Real announcements only (launches, market reports, partnerships, milestones). Plan-only mode → angle + cost presentation → approval → Vault-grounded draft in brand voice → review → distribution → tracking report. Framed internally as brand comms + AI-citation surface, never as link building.
- **P4 · Authority — Earned Authority Plan** *(upgrade of Explorer E6 — not net-new)*. The intelligence layer of #5–#8 completes E6: competitor authority metrics (DR/RD/link types), anchor & placement audit, topical cluster mapping, vulnerability targeting, signal replication analysis → phased 30-day sprint / 90-day arc (foundation → topical depth → expansion) executed exclusively through earned channels (P1 outreach, P3 news, internal linking, first-party topical clusters, agent E-E-A-T at scale) → ongoing anchor-diversity & velocity monitoring on the earned profile, with new-domain conservatism for young properties → roadmap with explicit sign-off. Budget consciousness survives as outreach capacity planning rather than credit allocation.

## Part 4 — Foundations & Fit

**One major new foundation ticket: cold-outreach email infrastructure.** P1/P2 need what the ads and GBP suites needed from their platforms — protected, professional sending capability: dedicated outreach domain(s) separate from liqwd.com (root-domain reputation is never gambled), SPF/DKIM/DMARC, gradual warm-up, an email-verification service, per-inbox daily caps and weekday scheduling enforced centrally (not per-prompt), and a send ledger (the change-ledger pattern applied to outreach: every send, bounce, reply, and opt-out logged).

**The Canadian overlay the source ignores: CASL.** Canada's anti-spam law is stricter than the US regime the source assumes. Commercial electronic messages require consent — for B2B realtor outreach, the workable basis is typically *implied consent via conspicuously published address* (realtor emails published in registries/brokerage sites, message relevant to their professional role) — plus mandatory sender identification, a functioning unsubscribe honored promptly, and record-keeping of the consent basis per contact. The P2 compliance lint must encode CASL alongside the no-false-claims check: consent-basis recorded for every prospect, ID block and unsubscribe present in every draft, suppression list enforced at the infrastructure layer. This is non-negotiable before the first send — penalties are real and LIQWD's whole posture is trust.

**Sequencing:** P2 first — it's the tool that grows LIQWD itself, and its insight table gets sharper as G1/V-suite data accumulates (works day one with manual research) → outreach infrastructure + CASL framework built alongside → P1 once there are pages and data assets worth pitching (market reports are the natural first Trophy Content) → P3 on the first real announcement → P4/E6 upgrade as the quarterly planning layer once P1 is producing.

**Cross-suite wiring:** V3 Trophy Content gaps → P1 pitch targets · P1/P3 earned coverage → V-suite citation tracking + E6 authority inputs · G1 + V-tracker data → P2 insight tables · P2 recruits → the entire agent-tier funnel · P3 announcements ← market-report content from the resale module · anchor/velocity monitor → E5-family reporting.
